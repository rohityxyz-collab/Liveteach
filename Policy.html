1. Privacy Policy

Welcome to Live Teach Pro Studio, an advanced digital education and smart board software suite created and published by HIDE Store (Developer: Rohit Yadav). This Privacy Policy explains how we gather, utilize, and safeguard your personal information when you access our mobile app and digital services.

🛡️ Absolute Data Protection Guarantee: Your educational content, lesson plans, slide decks, whiteboard drawings, and session recordings remain entirely private. We never trade, lease, or monetize your professional data with external commercial networks.

Information We Collect

  • Identity & Profile Data: We collect your name, email address, and primary teaching specialization when you establish an account to synchronize your cloud workspace.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure login credentials and session tokens are logged to protect your active license and enforce our single-device security guard.
  • Teaching Telemetry & Analytics: To provide accurate usage insights, we track instructional screen time, presentation counts, and recording durations.
  • Local Storage Sandbox: Presentation packages (`.liveteach`) and lecture video recordings are saved locally inside your device browser sandbox or internal app storage.

Authentication Protocol

Our application implements a streamlined, secure authentication workflow using direct Google Firebase Auth integration. Users authenticate via standard Gmail/Email & Password or 1-Tap Google Sign-In. No complex multi-factor OTP loops are required, ensuring instant, frictionless access for busy educators.
