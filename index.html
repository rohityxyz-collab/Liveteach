1. Privacy Policy

Welcome to Live Teach Pro Studio, an advanced digital education and smart board software suite created and published by HIDE Store (Developer: Rohit Yadav). This Privacy Policy explains how we gather, utilize, and safeguard your personal information when you access our mobile app and digital services.

🛡️ Absolute Data Protection Guarantee: Your educational content, lesson plans, slide decks, whiteboard drawings, and session recordings remain entirely private. We never trade, lease, or monetize your professional data with external commercial networks.

Information We Collect

  • Identity & Profile Data: We collect your name, email address (Gmail), and teaching profile when you register an account to synchronize your educational workspace.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure login credentials (email and password) and session tokens are processed securely to verify user identity and protect workspace access.
  • Teaching Telemetry & Analytics: To provide accurate usage insights, we track instructional screen time, presentation counts, and recording durations within the studio.
  • Local Storage Sandbox: Presentation packages (`.liveteach`) and lecture video recordings are saved locally inside your device browser sandbox or internal app storage.

Authentication Protocol

Our application implements a direct and secure authentication workflow using standard Email/Gmail and Password credentials via Google Firebase Auth. Users register with their email and chosen password, and log in directly using the same email and password. There are no OTPs (One-Time Passwords) or multi-step verification loops required, ensuring instant and straightforward access to teaching tools.
